    subtitle: Change the registration certification date by updating

     About
     Every registration certificate is required to be renewed not more than 60 days before the date of its expiry of the registration for its continuous use.

  - type: HeroSection
    title: Guidelines
    size: Small
    text: >
     Apply for renewal of certificate of registration in Form 25 to the Registering Authority in whose jurisdiction the vehicle is, not more than 60 days before the date of its expiry <br>
     Pay the due taxes on vehicle, if any <br>
     Pay appropriate fee as specified in Rule 81 of the Central Motor Vehicle Rules 1989
